# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and equitable project community. Participants should engage honestly, respectfully, and constructively, give and receive feedback in good faith, respect boundaries and privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, publication of private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, community events, and when someone officially represents ProofIssue.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Until a dedicated community contact is published, report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or the hosting platform's private reporting tools. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, issue warnings, or suspend access in proportion to the behavior and community safety needs. Reports should be handled as privately, promptly, consistently, and fairly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
